Welcome to the Tinder for Movies

Cine-Swipe is a movie finding app inspired by platforms like Tinder and Hinge. Users can create lobbies and are shown a stream of movies one at a time and can swipe to like or dislike each title based on their interest.

The core feature of Cine-Swipe is the “match” system. When all users collectively like the same movie, it becomes a global match and is revealed to everyone in the app. This creates an easy way to turn the endless scrolling and searching of a group movie night into a fast and fun activity to find a movie that everyone will enjoy.
